Home · About

Making places more beautiful for nearly 20 years.

Snellman Kitchens is an Aurora-based kitchen studio — designers, craftsmen and installers under one roof, obsessed with the details you'll touch every day.

Our story

Beside your dreams, since day one.

We started with a simple belief: a kitchen isn't furniture — it's where life happens. Nearly two decades later, that belief still drives every measurement, every joint, every finish we choose. From our showroom on Industrial Parkway in Aurora, we design and build custom kitchens for families across the Greater Toronto Area.

No middlemen, no shortcuts. You talk to the people who design your kitchen, and the people who build it. That's why our clients send their neighbours to us.

The Workshop

What we stand by

Three promises, kept daily.

Elegant

Design that doesn't shout. Balanced proportions, honest materials, and finishes chosen to age gracefully.

On Time

A schedule you can plan your life around. We start when we say, and we finish when we say.

1st Quality

Cabinets built to the millimetre, hardware that feels right, and a final walkthrough we don't rush.

Yours

Every kitchen is drawn around your home and your habits — never pulled from a catalogue.
