Mid-transformation · Aurora

Chapter 01 — The Starting Point

It begins with a kitchen that no longer fits

Every project starts the same way: a kitchen that works against the people who use it — dark corners, wasted space, finishes from another decade. We measure everything, listen to how you actually cook and gather, and design around your life, not a catalogue.

Then the transformation begins. Floors protected, timelines set, and our installers treat your home like their own while the old kitchen comes out and the new one takes shape.

The details decide

Chapter 02 — The Craft

Built to the millimetre, finished to be lived in

Cabinets are built to your exact dimensions — never cut down from stock sizes. Soft-close hardware as standard, seams you can't find with your fingertip, and lighting planned like it matters. Because it does.

  • Shaker, high gloss, matte and classic — four signature styles
  • Warm under-cabinet lighting designed into the plan
  • Brass, matte black and chrome hardware, sampled in person

Chapter 03 — The Reveal
